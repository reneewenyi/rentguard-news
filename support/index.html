常见问题

要注册账号吗？不用。你的房子、照片和记录只存在你的手机里。

换手机或删掉 App，资料还在吗？资料只在这台手机里，删掉 App 会一起删掉。换手机前可以在「我的」→「导出全部资料」保存一份。

App 里的规定准不准？每条都附官方出处，并每月核对一次。规定会变，重要的事请以出处原文为准。发现不对请告诉我们，改正会公开记录。

这是法律意见吗？不是。App 提供一般信息和工具。有纠纷请咨询律师或免费法律援助（例如 Pro Bono SG）。

你们收中介或房东的钱吗？不收。我们不推荐、也不担保任何中介、房东、律师、师傅或公司。

紧急情况：火警、急救 995；报警 999；闻到煤气 1800 752 1800（City Energy）。

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. Your homes, photos and records stay on your phone.

If I change phones or delete the app? Data lives only on this phone and is removed with the app. Use Me → Export all data before switching.

Is the content accurate? Each item links to its official source and is reviewed monthly. Rules change — the source is what counts. Tell us about errors; corrections are logged publicly.

Is this legal advice? No. For disputes, see a lawyer or a free legal clinic such as Pro Bono SG.

Do you take money from agents or landlords? No. We don't recommend or endorse any agent, landlord, lawyer, tradesperson or company.

Emergency: fire / ambulance 995; police 999; gas leak 1800 752 1800 (City Energy).
