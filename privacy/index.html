隐私政策

租房守护（RentGuard）按新加坡《个人数据保护法》（PDPA）处理你的数据。下面写的是 App 实际做的事。

数据存在哪里

房子资料、合同照片、验房和看房的照片视频、记录、提醒，都只存在这台手机的 App 存储里。开发者看不到这些内容。

删除一套房子，会同时删除它的照片和视频。卸载 App 会删除 App 里的文件；iOS 可能在卸载后保留钥匙串里的内容（比如 API key），要彻底删掉，请卸载前先在设置里清空。

什么会发出手机

查中介：中介注册号发给新加坡政府开放数据平台 data.gov.sg 查询公开名录。

查房租：组屋版把镇名、房型发给 data.gov.sg；公寓版查询 URA 的公开租约数据。

地址：帖子里读到的邮编会发给新加坡政府的 OneMap 查询门牌地址。

帖子链接：你贴的链接只存在手机里，方便你以后打开原帖；App 不会替你去打开或下载帖子页面。截图里的文字在手机里识别，不上传。

租房快讯：从 reneewenyi.github.io（GitHub Pages）下载，只下载，不发送你的信息。

除此之外，App 不收集使用统计，不放广告，不把数据卖给或分享给任何人。

证件号码

App 不会要求你提供身份证、FIN、护照或准证号码，也不用它们登录。

位置和照片

在 App 里直接拍照时，会记录拍摄时间和位置，用来证明照片是何时何地拍的。位置只存在手机里，只出现在你自己生成的报告里。

照片里可能有房东、室友或中介的信息。这些照片只供你为自己的租房留存证据，分享报告前请想一想对方的隐私。

数据来源与许可

组屋出租登记、中介公开名录来自 data.gov.sg；私宅租约来自 URA Data Service；地址来自 OneMap。以上按《新加坡开放数据许可》（data.gov.sg/open-data-licence）使用。

App 根据这些公开数据和公开规则整理出的结论只是参考，不是政府或任何机构的官方结论，也不是法律意见。

你的权利

数据都在你手里：你可以随时查看、修改或删除。

Privacy policy

RentGuard handles your data under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). This page describes what the app actually does.

Where your data is stored

Home details, lease photos, inspection and viewing photos and videos, records and reminders are stored only in the app’s storage on this phone. The developer cannot see them.

Deleting a home also deletes its photos and videos. Uninstalling the app deletes the app’s files; iOS may keep keychain items (such as an API key) after uninstalling, so clear them in Settings first.

What leaves your phone

Agent check: the agent registration number is sent to data.gov.sg, the Singapore Government’s open data platform, to look up the public register.

Rent check: for HDB, the town and flat type are sent to data.gov.sg; for condos, URA’s public rental contract data is queried.

Address: a postal code read from a post is sent to the Singapore Government’s OneMap to find the address.

Post links: links you paste are kept on your phone so you can reopen the post; the app does not open or download the post page for you. Text in screenshots is read on your phone and not uploaded.

Rental news: downloaded from reneewenyi.github.io (GitHub Pages); nothing about you is sent.

Nothing else. The app collects no usage analytics, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data.

ID numbers

The app never asks for your NRIC, FIN, passport or pass number and does not use them to sign in.

Location and photos

Photos taken in the app record the time and place, to show when and where they were taken. Location stays on your phone and appears only in reports you create.

Photos may show your landlord, housemates or agent. Keep them as evidence for your own tenancy, and think about their privacy before sharing a report.

Data sources and licence

HDB renting-out approvals and the CEA public register come from data.gov.sg; private rental contracts from URA Data Service; addresses from OneMap. They are used under the Singapore Open Data Licence (data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).

What the app works out from public data and published rules is a reference only. It is not an official conclusion of any government agency, and it is not legal advice.

Your rights

Your data is in your hands: you can view, change or delete it at any time.

For questions, to withdraw consent or to complain about personal data, contact the data protection officer email below.
